# Product Opportunity Evaluation

## Scope and inputs
Use for an opportunity review or a concrete route decision. Accept the brief, search coverage, comparison, user evidence and constraints; actual experiment results are optional. If research is missing, make the review conditional and disclose it.

## Tools
Read artifacts. Verify current capabilities, costs or licenses only when they affect the decision; use primary technical sources. Planning an experiment is not authorization to execute it.

## Method
1. Read the [evaluation framework](../../references/product-evaluation-framework.md). Calibrate role, situation, problem, desired outcome, current alternative and evidence.
2. Review six dimensions: demand reality; scale/reach; supply/feasibility; competition/differentiation; business or maintenance sustainability; validation/decision. Separate evidence, assumptions, risks and confidence; seek counterevidence.
3. Include saved prompts, native chat, manual workflows and doing nothing as alternatives. Explain switching value and whether it lasts. Open-source and personal projects need not adopt a paid business model.
4. Turn material claims into falsifiable assumptions; identify the one most likely to invalidate the project. Present the strongest reasonable supporting case before challenging it.
5. Summarize the cheapest test: hypothesis, method, behavior metric, success/failure/inconclusive criteria, kill condition and next action. Detailed operational design belongs to validation-plan.
6. Choose exactly one recommendation from the framework. Include rationale, confidence, biggest risk, strongest evidence and next step. Thin evidence usually calls for validation; unknown licenses do not establish fork permission.

## Deliverable
A **Product Opportunity Review + Decision**, including calibrated problem, six reviews, assumption register and one primary recommendation. Follow the [contracts](../../references/io-contracts.md).

End with knowns, critical unknowns and next action. If the user overrides the recommendation, preserve the evidence and risk while honoring their chosen scope. Do not generate a full PRD or invent experiment results.
